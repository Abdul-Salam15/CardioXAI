What is CardioXAI?

Most cardiovascular risk tools require laboratory inputs — cholesterol readings, fasting glucose, ECG results — that are inaccessible to millions of people without a clinical appointment. CardioXAI was designed around a different premise: that behavioral and demographic data alone, when processed through an explainable machine learning pipeline, can provide actionable risk awareness. Every prediction comes with a SHAP-based breakdown showing exactly which factors drove the score — not just a number, but a reason. LIME (Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations) is used alongside SHAP to provide a second independent explanation layer, increasing interpretability robustness.

How does the model work?

Three models were trained on the CDC Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) 2022 dataset — over 246,000 adult observations across 21 self-reported features. Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost were evaluated independently; the two best-performing models — Logistic Regression and XGBoost — form a weighted consensus ensemble used for final predictions.

When the ensemble members disagree significantly, the system flags a Low Confidence result and routes the user toward professional consultation rather than acting on an uncertain score.

Who built this?

CardioXAI was developed as an undergraduate academic research project in applied machine learning and explainable AI. It is a proof-of-concept system — not a registered medical device, not clinically validated, and not a replacement for a doctor. Its purpose is to demonstrate that transparent, no-laboratory cardiovascular risk screening is technically feasible and to contribute a reproducible framework to the responsible AI in healthcare literature.

Important Disclaimer

CardioXAI is a research prototype and does not constitute medical advice. Risk scores are statistical estimates derived from population-level data and may not reflect your individual clinical status. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making any decisions about your health.
